# Code of Conduct

founders.coffee exists to bring founders together in good faith, and the people who build it should
be able to work together the same way.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part in a project space agrees to follow it. It is also
available [in French](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/fr/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/). Version
3.0 has no Arabic translation yet; [version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/ar/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
does, and says the same in substance.

## Where it applies

It applies in every space this project runs: this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and commit messages, and anywhere else you represent the project, online or in person.

The founders.coffee service, and the meetups organised through it, follow the service's own terms,
published on [founders.coffee](https://founders.coffee). Report a problem at a meetup there, not here.

## Reporting a problem

Email **contact@founders.coffee** with "Code of conduct" in the subject. Say what happened, where
(links help), when, and who was involved. You may report something that happened to someone else.

The maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) act as the Covenant's community
moderators. They read reports privately, and never share a reporter's identity without consent. If
your report concerns a maintainer, say so: that person will take no part in handling it. You can
also report content to GitHub directly, through its reporting tools.

## What happens next

We aim to acknowledge a report within a week. We look into it, hear from the people involved where
that is safe, and respond as the Covenant describes, with the aim of repairing harm. That response
can range from a private conversation to a permanent ban. We tell the reporter the outcome, as far
as privacy allows.
